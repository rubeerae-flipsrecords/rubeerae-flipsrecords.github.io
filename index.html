About This Place

This website is my own little digital scrapbook. It's somewhere to put the things that don't quite belong anywhere else.

Music, writing, photographs, collections, observations, memories, projects, and whatever else I decide deserves its own tiny corner of the internet.

There isn't really supposed to be a single theme here. It's intentionally cluttered. Think of it like opening an old drawer and finding twenty interesting things you forgot you owned.
